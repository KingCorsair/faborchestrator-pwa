The third row is the one a person experiences, because FabOrchestrator's own client
always fetches the conversation list on the way in. The second row is what that row
used to cost: before WP8 the proof was fetched again, at the moment it was needed.

The ownership check (WP4) is the only work the gateway does that costs an upstream
round trip. What it reads is this account's conversation list — **211 rows,
55 KB**, growing with the account's history — and since WP8 it reads it
from the copy that went past on the way in rather than fetching it again.
